Every game card shows the side the bot thinks is more likely to win, with its price check: VALUE = pays more than the real chance, FAIR = about right, OVERPRICED = the book is taking a big cut ("Pick only", no stake). Bet sizes: 1 unit = 2% of the paper bankroll (it grows as picks win). Best bets are sized by edge, 0.5u–3u (capped at 1u when only one sharp book prices it). A red ⚠ means a key player on that team was ruled out after the pick was posted. Best bet = your book pays at least 2% more than the sharp books' fair price (Pinnacle, devigged), odds 1.50–2.30. 1.5 units when the edge is 4%+ and 2+ sharp books agree. Lean = solid favourite priced about fair, half a unit. Parlays are half a unit and usually have no real edge: they're for fun. Don't take a pick if the odds drop below its "min odds". Paper-trade until the record proves itself. Gambling has real risk; never bet money you need.
